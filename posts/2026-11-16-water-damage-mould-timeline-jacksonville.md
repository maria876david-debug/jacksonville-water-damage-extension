# Water Damage and Mould: The Timeline Nobody Tells You About

Mould does not wait for a convenient day. In warm, damp conditions it can start growing within 24 to 48 hours of water damage, often inside walls and under floors where you cannot see it. That is why the first day matters more than the first week: water extraction, drying and airflow in the first 24 hours decide whether a small leak stays a small job.

What to do first: stop the water source, remove standing water, move wet belongings to dry air, and get fans and dehumidifiers running. Do not paint over a damp patch and do not wait for the smell to go away on its own - by the time you can smell mould, it has already settled in.
