# What to Photograph First After Water Damage in Jacksonville

After water damage, your phone is your best tool - but only if you photograph the right things before anything is moved. Start wide: every room, from the doorway, so the full extent is visible. Then go close: the source of the water, the waterline on the walls, soaked flooring and furniture, and any belongings that are damaged. Photograph serial numbers and labels on damaged appliances and electronics. Then photograph the cleanup itself - the water being removed, the drying equipment in place - because insurers ask how quickly the damage was dealt with. Do all of this before the first mop touches the floor; once cleanup starts, the evidence is gone.
