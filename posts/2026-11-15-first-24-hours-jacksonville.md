# The First 24 Hours Decide What Water Damage Costs in Jacksonville

Water damage has a clock on it, and in Jacksonville it runs fast. Mold can begin forming within 24 to 48 hours of water exposure, and restoration that starts within the first day typically costs less than a job delayed past 48 hours. A small, contained leak caught early usually takes 3 to 5 days for extraction and drying; a larger job, especially where mold has developed, can take 1 to 3 weeks including repairs.

Here is the surprise most homeowners miss: hurricanes are not the main cause. Most water damage claims in Jacksonville come from plumbing failures, not storms — a supply line failing behind a kitchen cabinet at 2 AM, a water heater leaking while you are away for a week. Riverside and San Marco face storm-surge risk from the St. Johns River; Westside and Northside flood more from drainage limits; Springfield and Downtown carry older plumbing that raises the risk of slow leaks behind walls.

Two things to know before you call anyone. First, mitigation and restoration are different jobs: mitigation is the immediate extraction and drying that stops damage spreading; restoration is repairing or replacing what was damaged afterwards. Most jobs need both. Second, a sudden, accidental event — a pipe bursting without warning — is typically covered under a standard policy, while gradual leaks are usually excluded. Documentation from day one is the best protection for your claim: every job is photographed and logged, and you get a written estimate before work begins.
