# Does Homeowners Insurance Cover Flood Damage in Jacksonville? Usually Not

This is one of the most common and costly misunderstandings in Florida: a standard homeowners insurance policy generally does not cover flood damage. Water that rises from the ground — storm surge, an overwhelmed drainage system, a swollen river — needs a separate flood insurance policy, typically through the National Flood Insurance Program or a private flood carrier. If your Jacksonville home took on water from outside rather than from a plumbing failure, your standard policy likely will not apply. Confirm it with your agent before storm season, not during one.

If flooding does happen, two habits protect your claim. First, photograph everything before anything is moved or cleaned — flood adjusters need the water line height on your walls, and your photos support both an insurance claim and any FEMA application after a declared disaster. Second, get extraction started as fast as you safely can; restoration professionals document room-by-room moisture logs that become part of your proof of loss.
