# Water Damage in a Rental: Who Pays, Tenant or Landlord?

Water damage in a rental raises one question before any other: who pays? The short answer is that it depends on what caused the water. If the leak came from the building - a burst pipe, a roof leak, a failed water heater - the landlord is usually responsible for the repair and the drying. If the tenant caused it - an overflowing bath left running, a blocked drain they ignored - the tenant can be liable. Either way, the first job is the same: stop the water, photograph everything before anything is moved, and tell the landlord in writing the same day. Tenants should also know that the landlord's insurance covers the building, not the tenant's belongings - that is what renter's insurance is for. Photograph first, report fast, and keep a copy of everything you send.
